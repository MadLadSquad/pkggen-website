## 1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how **pkggen** ("we", "us", or "our"), a project maintained by [MadLadSquad](https://madladsquad.com) and [Heapforge](https://heapforge.com), collects, uses, and protects technical and personal information when you visit and use our website at [https://pkggen.madladsquad.com/](https://pkggen.madladsquad.com/).

We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting personal data in compliance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 - "GDPR")**, the **UK General Data Protection Regulation**, and the **EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC)**.

Our website is built on the principles of **privacy by design** and **data minimization**: we do not collect personal information, tracking profiles, or analytics identifiers, and we only process the technical data necessary to deliver static website content securely and reliably.

This website is a static site. It contains **no JavaScript**, loads **no third-party resources**, and makes **no requests to any domain other than its own**.

Cloudflare acts as both our hosting provider and our content delivery network. Pages are served from the Cloudflare edge location nearest to you.

When you access our website, Cloudflare's servers automatically process and temporarily store technical data in server log files. This standard technical transmission data includes:

We have no access to, and do not retain, raw request logs for this website.

For further information on how Cloudflare processes data, please refer to the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and the [Cloudflare subprocessors list](https://www.cloudflare.com/gdpr/subprocessors/).

## 4. Assets and External Content

**This website loads no third-party resources whatsoever.** Every asset required to render a page — stylesheets, web fonts, images, and icons — is served from `pkggen.madladsquad.com` itself. Your browser makes no connection to any other domain when viewing this site.

Specifically, the following are self-hosted on our own domain:

- **The Ubuntu typeface** (`ubuntu-*.woff2`), used for all text. It is *not* loaded from Google Fonts; no request is made to `fonts.googleapis.com` or `fonts.gstatic.com`.

- **Syntax highlighting for code samples**, which is applied when the site is built rather than by a script in your browser. No highlighting library is downloaded from a CDN.
- **All images and icons** (the pkggen logo, favicon, and social preview image).

## 5. Cookies, Local Storage, and Tracking

### No Cookies Are Set by Us

**This website does not use cookies.** We do not place:

- Tracking cookies
- Advertising or marketing cookies
- Analytics cookies
- Session or authentication cookies

### Cloudflare Security Cookies

Depending on the security features enabled on our Cloudflare zone, Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie such as `__cf_bm` (bot management) or `cf_clearance` (challenge clearance). These cookies exist solely to distinguish automated traffic from human visitors and to protect the site from abuse. They contain no personal profile, are not used for analytics or advertising, and are **exempt from the consent requirement** under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive as strictly necessary for the provision of the service you requested. See the [Cloudflare cookie documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/) for details.

### No Local Storage Tracking

The site ships no JavaScript at all, and therefore cannot and does not use browser storage technologies (`localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, or `IndexedDB`) to track users, store behavioral profiles, or persist unique identifiers across visits.

### No Web Analytics

We do not use third-party tracking or web analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Matomo, or Facebook Pixel), and we do not use Cloudflare Web Analytics.

### Why There Is No Cookie Consent Banner

Under the **EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC)** and relevant national implementations, a cookie consent banner is required only when a website uses non-essential cookies or stores/accesses information on the user's terminal equipment.

Because this website sets no cookies of its own and uses no local tracking mechanisms — and because the only cookies that may be set are Cloudflare's strictly necessary security cookies — **no cookie consent banner is legally required**.

## 8. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the GDPR (Articles 15 through 22 and Article 77), you have the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR):** You have the right to request confirmation as to whether we process personal data concerning you and to receive information about that processing.
- **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR):** You have the right to obtain the correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR):** You have the right to request the erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") when statutory conditions are met.
- **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR):** You have the right to request the restriction of processing under specific legal conditions.
- **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR):** You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR):** You have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of personal data based on **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR** (legitimate interests).
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint (Art. 77 GDPR):** If you believe that our processing of your personal data violates data protection legislation, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority in the EU/EEA member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

To exercise any of these rights, please send an email to <contact@madladsquad.com>.
